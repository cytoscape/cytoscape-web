Cytoscape Web Privacy Policy
===========================
<a id="cytoscape_web_privacy_policy"> </a>

**Last Updated: October 27th, 2026**

At Cytoscape Web, accessible from [https://web.cytoscape.org](https://web.cytoscape.org), your privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our website and services. By using our platform, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

## Information and Data

### 1. Information We Collect

Cytoscape Web does not ask for your name, email address or other contact details. It handles the following information:

- **Your workspace.** The networks, styles, filters and settings you work with are stored in your web browser, on your own computer. They leave your computer only when you save them to the Network Data Exchange (NDEx), export them, send them to an app or service you chose, or when a crash report is sent (see below).
- **Your NDEx account.** When you sign in, you sign in to your NDEx account. Networks and workspaces you save are stored in NDEx. Please refer to the [NDEx privacy policy](https://home.ndexbio.org/disclaimer-license/) for details.
- **Crash reports.** When Cytoscape Web stops with an error and shows its error page, it sends a crash report to a server operated by the Cytoscape team at NDEx (`www.ndexbio.org`). A crash report contains the error message and its technical details, the page address, the Cytoscape Web version, your browser type, platform, language, window size and memory use, and a copy of the data Cytoscape Web stores in your browser: your whole workspace if it is 10 MB or smaller, otherwise only the network you were viewing. Crash reports are sent unless you opt out (see section 5).
- **Usage statistics.** Cytoscape Web uses Google Analytics on every visit, and sends usage events if you accept cookies, as described in section 3.
- **Preferences.** Cytoscape Web stores a few preferences in your browser, such as your cookie and crash report choices and whether you have seen the welcome tour.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

Cytoscape Web uses information in the following ways:

- To provide, operate, and maintain our website and services.
- To improve and personalize your user experience.
- To diagnose and fix errors, using crash reports.
- To communicate with you regarding updates, support, and other service-related information.
- To analyze how our website is used and to gather statistical data to enhance our platform.
- To prevent fraudulent activities and ensure security.

### 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your data in the following circumstances:

**Service Providers**: We may share your information with trusted third-party providers who help us operate our platform, such as analytics providers. For example, we use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics loads on every visit, whether or not you accept cookies, and collects information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. Google processes this data on our behalf to help us understand how users interact with Cytoscape Web. You can learn more about how Google uses your data by visiting [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Users can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

**Usage Events**: If you accept cookies, Cytoscape Web also sends Google Analytics a named event when you open a network, export an image, save a network to NDEx, or run an app. An event records only the kind of action: where a network came from (NDEx, a file, a link, a web address or a sample) and its format, the image format, the kind of NDEx save, and the type of app, with its name only for apps and services that come with Cytoscape Web. Events never include network names or IDs, file names, your NDEx account, or the address of an app or service you added. If you decline cookies or do not answer the cookie banner, no events are sent.

**Apps and Services You Choose**: When you install a third-party "app" or add a "service app" to extend Cytoscape Web, that app or service can receive the network data it works on. A service app runs on its own server, operated by its provider.

**Large Language Models**: When you use **Analysis → Run LLM Query**, Cytoscape Web sends the gene symbols you query and the prompt to the language model provider you choose (for example OpenAI). The API key you enter is kept in memory only and is not stored in your browser.

**Legal Compliance**: We may disclose your information when required by law or in response to legal processes (e.g., subpoenas, court orders).

### 4. Data Security

Data you save to NDEx is stored in NDEx. Please refer to the NDEx privacy policy for details about the measures taken to protect it. Data stored in your browser is protected by your browser and your computer. Anyone who uses the same browser profile can open it. **Data → Clear Local Database** deletes it.

## Rights and Choices

### 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access**: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction**: You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Deletion**: You may request the deletion of your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.
- **Withdrawal of Consent**: If we process your data based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

**Opting out of crash reports**: The cookie banner at the bottom of the page has an **Opt out** link. Click it to stop crash reports from this browser; click **Enable crash reports** to turn them back on. Your choice is kept for one year. After you click **Accept**, the banner stays hidden for 150 days; to see it again before then, clear the cookies for this site.

**Cookies**: Clicking **Decline** in the cookie banner removes the cookies that Cytoscape Web can read for this site at that moment. Cookies set for a wider domain (such as `cytoscape.org`) or marked HttpOnly may remain; you can remove them in your browser's settings.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at [support@cytoscape.org](mailto:support@cytoscape.org).

### 6. Third-Party Links

Cytoscape Web may contain links to external websites that are not operated by us. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

### 7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently gathered personal data from a child, we will take steps to delete it.

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. Any updates will be posted on this page with the revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed of any changes.
